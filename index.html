It is a client, not a service

This is the part to understand before you install it. Fluxwave does not generate anything and does not own a GPU. It sends a workflow to a ComfyUI server that belongs to you and shows you what comes back. If your server is off, Fluxwave does nothing at all.

  1. You already run ComfyUI — on a desktop in the next room, a workstation at the office, or a box you rent.
  2. You point Fluxwave at it once, by address. No account, no sign-up, no relay of mine in between.
  3. You tap a template. Fluxwave builds the graph, submits the job and tracks it.
  4. The result comes back into a proper library, with the slider, ready to save or share.

The templates

Each one is a ComfyUI graph you would otherwise wire by hand, reduced to a single tap — and each only does what the models installed on your own server can do.

Native everywhere, for real

Not one web view stretched across five devices. On the Mac it is a document-shaped app with a proper library, drag-and-drop in and out, and the full window in use. On iPhone and iPad it is a touch app that respects the keyboard and the pencil. There are builds for Apple TV and Vision Pro too.

What it costs

Free covers connecting your server, three basic templates and the on-device basics — enough to know whether it fits how you work. Premium adds the full template pack, batch processing, the 4K upscale, the model manager and the before/after export video. It is sold monthly, annually with a seven-day trial, or once as a Lifetime purchase; prices are shown in the app in your own currency.

Honest about the limits
  • No server, no app. There is no cloud tier and no credits to buy. If you do not run ComfyUI, Fluxwave has nothing to talk to — please do not buy it hoping otherwise.
  • Quality is your server's. The checkpoints, LoRAs and VRAM are yours. A template cannot outrun the models you installed.
  • Speed is your server's too. Fluxwave shows the real queue and the real progress; it cannot make a job finish sooner.
  • No account, no sync. Your library is local to each device.
