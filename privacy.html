Fluxwave Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Fluxwave is an AI image studio that connects to your own image-generation server (ComfyUI). We host nothing and we do not collect, store, or transmit your images or prompts to our own servers.

What stays on your device

  • Server address & access token — stored only in your device Keychain. They are used to talk directly to the server you choose.
  • Prompts and generated images — sent directly from your device to your own server, and saved locally / to your Photos when you choose. Fluxwave never receives them.

Content & safety

Fluxwave includes a safety filter and in-app reporting/blocking. You are responsible for the content you generate on your own server. Do not create content that is illegal or that depicts real people without consent. The app is rated 17+.

Data we collect

None. No analytics servers, no account system.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment information.
