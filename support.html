Fluxwave Support

Fluxwave turns your own ComfyUI server into a one-tap AI image studio on your Apple devices.

Getting started

  1. In Settings → Servers, add your ComfyUI server URL and access token.
  2. Pick a template (e.g. Selfie → Anime, Restore Old Photo), add a prompt or photo, and run it.
  3. Compare the result with the before/after slider and save or share it.

Frequently asked

Do I need a server? Yes — Fluxwave is a client for your own ComfyUI server (local or cloud). You run and pay for that compute.

Is my content private? Yes — prompts and images go straight to your server. See our Privacy Policy.

What does Premium add? The full premium template pack, batch, 4K upscale, model manager and before/after export video.

Restore a purchase? Settings → Restore Purchases.
